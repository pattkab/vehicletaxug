What a vehicle costs to clear in Uganda

    Questions people ask before clearing a car

    How much is import duty on a car in Uganda?

    URA’s published example uses 25% of the CIF customs value. VAT at 18% is then charged on the CIF plus that duty. Withholding tax is 6% of CIF, the infrastructure levy is 1.5%, and the import declaration fee is 1%. A passenger car of 9 years or more also pays an environmental levy of 50% of CIF. The rate table lists each line.

    Which Uganda Revenue Authority valuation is used?

    The Used Motor Vehicle Database reviewed on 1 August 2026, with 31,872 CIF figures. On 7 November 2026 that was still the newest file in URA’s valuation-guide archive. Common models are listed on the URA values page.

    Does URA tax the price on the invoice?

    For a used vehicle, no. URA uses the valuation database. An invoice below the guide value does not lower the tax. The FAQ explains the rule.

    Can I import a car older than 15 years?

    A passenger car of 15 years or more from the year of manufacture is not allowed. The 13-year proposal was withdrawn in April 2026. Details are on the age and levy page.
