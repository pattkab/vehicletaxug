On 7 November 2026 the newest download in URA’s valuation-guide archive was still “Used Motor Vehicle Database 1st August 2026”. The calculator holds all 31,872 numeric rows from that file. The table below is a short list of models commonly cleared in Uganda, not a substitute for searching the chassis code.

Small passenger cars

A 2016 passenger car imported in 2026 is 10 years old, so the 50% environmental levy applies. A 2018 passenger car in the same year is 8 years old and does not. Search the full description if the engine or body differs from the row here.

The Hilux row is a goods-vehicle heading, so the calculator starts it on the 20% levy band. A Hiace or Prado on a passenger heading uses the 50% band from 9 years. Confirm the HS code on the estimate before you treat the levy as final.
